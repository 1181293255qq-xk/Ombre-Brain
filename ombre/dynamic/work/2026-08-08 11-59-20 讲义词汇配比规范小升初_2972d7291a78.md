小羽+DeepSeek共同确认，后续所有小升初讲义词汇按此规范出词：

配比：80%小学高频词 + 20%初一前三单元校园词（library/dining hall/building/gym）

同类动词轮换替换清单（避免例句重复）：
- go fishing → go swimming / go hiking / go shopping
- clean the room → clean the desk / clean the yard
- watch a movie → watch TV / watch a game
- study English → study math / study in the library
- have a lesson → have dinner / have a party
